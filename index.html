Capabilities

Why Choose ÄKTA™

Industry-leading chromatography technology backed by decades of innovation.

UNICORN™ Software

Intuitive control software with method wizards, real-time monitoring, and comprehensive data analysis. GLP/GMP compliant.

Reproducibility

Automated and reproducible protein purification with precise flow control and gradient elution for consistent results every time.

Multi-Mode Chromatography

Support for affinity, ion exchange, size exclusion, hydrophobic interaction, and multimodal chromatography techniques.

High Performance

Operating pressures up to 20 MPa (2900 psi) with flow rates from µL/min to 150 mL/min for versatile applications.

Regulatory Compliance

Built-in support for GLP and GMP environments with audit trails, electronic signatures, and 21 CFR Part 11 compliance.

Modular Design

Customize your system with interchangeable modules. Scale from microscale to process development as your needs evolve.
